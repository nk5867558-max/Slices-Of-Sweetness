🥭 Mango Tart 🥭

Sweet, creamy, and filled with juicy tropical mango flavour! 💛

🥭 ⭐ 🥭 ✨ 🥭 💛 🥧 🌼 🥭 ✨ 💛 🥧

A delicious mango tart with a crisp, buttery pastry base, smooth creamy filling, and sweet, juicy mango slices. This tropical dessert is bright, refreshing, and full of delicious mango flavour. Every bite is buttery, creamy, fruity, and perfect for a sunny afternoon treat! 🥭💛

🥭 Key Ingredients 🥭

Ingredients Measurements Use
Fresh Mangoes 2–3 Main flavour and topping
All-Purpose Flour 1½ cups Creates the pastry base
Butter ½ cup Adds richness to the pastry
White Sugar ½ cup Adds sweetness
Eggs 2 Helps create the creamy filling
Heavy Cream 1 cup Creates a smooth and creamy filling
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp Adds extra flavour
Lemon Juice 1 tbsp Adds a fresh, zesty flavour
Buttery pastry, creamy filling, and sweet juicy mangoes come together to create a bright, tropical, and delicious dessert! 🥭🥧✨

🥭 How to Make Mango Tart 🥭

Step 1: Prepare the Pastry

Mix the flour, sugar, and butter together until the mixture forms a soft dough. Press the dough evenly into a tart tin.

Step 2: Bake the Pastry

Prick the base gently with a fork and bake at 180°C for about 15–20 minutes, or until the pastry is lightly golden. Allow it to cool completely.

Step 3: Make the Creamy Filling

Whisk the eggs, cream, sugar, vanilla extract, and lemon juice together until smooth and well combined.

Step 4: Add the Mango

Pour the creamy filling into the cooled pastry shell. Slice the fresh mangoes into thin pieces and arrange them beautifully over the top.

Step 5: Bake and Cool

Bake the tart at 160°C for approximately 25–30 minutes, or until the filling is set. Allow the tart to cool before serving.

✨ Enjoy a delicious slice of creamy mango tart with sweet, juicy mangoes and a buttery pastry base! 🥭🥧💛✨
