🍓 Strawberry Shortcake 🍓

Soft, fluffy, creamy, and packed with sweet strawberries!

🍓 ⭐ 🍓 ✨ 🍓 ⭐ 🎀 💗 🍓 ✨ 💗 🎀

A cloud-like cake layered with pillowy soft sponge and rich whipped cream, crowned with fresh, juicy strawberries. Every bite melts effortlessly — light, creamy, and perfectly sweet. The softness of the cake, richness of the cream, and bright burst of strawberries create a dessert that feels magical. One slice and you'll know why it's pure heaven. 🍓🍰✨

🍓 Key Ingredients 🍓

Ingredients Measurements Use
Strawberries 2 pounds (900g) For topping and syrup
All-Purpose Flour 2 cups Makes the shortcake
Baking Powder 1 tbsp Helps the shortcake rise
Sugar ½ cup Sweetens the shortcake
Egg 1 Binds the ingredients together
Heavy Cream 1 cup Makes the whipped cream topping
Butter ½ cup Adds richness to the shortcake
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp Adds extra flavour
Fresh strawberries, fluffy shortcake, and sweet whipped cream come together to make this dessert extra yummy! 🍓🍰

🍓 How to Make Strawberry Shortcake 🍓

Step 1: Prepare the Strawberries

Wash and slice the strawberries. Mix some of the strawberries with sugar and allow them to sit for 5–10 minutes until they become juicy and create a sweet syrup. Keep some fresh strawberries aside for decorating.

Step 2: Make the Shortcake

Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl. Rub in the butter until the mixture becomes crumbly. Add the egg and a little cream, then mix until a soft dough forms.

Step 3: Bake the Shortcake

Place the dough onto a baking tray and shape it into a thick round. Bake for about 12–15 minutes or until the shortcake is golden brown. Allow it to cool before assembling.

Step 4: Whip the Cream

Beat the heavy cream until it becomes light and fluffy. Add a little sugar and vanilla extract and continue mixing until soft peaks form.

Step 5: Assemble

Slice the shortcake in half. Add a layer of whipped cream and sweet strawberries with their syrup. Place the top half back on and add more whipped cream and fresh strawberries on top.

✨ Enjoy layers of buttery shortcake, fluffy whipped cream, and sweet juicy strawberries! 🍓🍰✨
