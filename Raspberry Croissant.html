🍓🥐 Raspberry Croissant 🥐🍓

Flaky, buttery, and filled with sweet raspberry goodness! 💗

🍓 ⭐ 🍓 ✨ 🍓 💗 🥐 🌸 🍓 ✨ 💗 🥐

A delicious raspberry croissant made with flaky, golden pastry and sweet, juicy raspberries. The buttery layers are crisp on the outside and soft inside, while the raspberry filling adds a bright, fruity flavour. Every bite is buttery, fruity, and perfect for breakfast or an afternoon treat! 🍓🥐✨

🍓 Key Ingredients 🍓

Ingredients Measurements Use
Fresh Raspberries 1½ cups Main fruity flavour and filling
Puff Pastry 1 sheet Creates the flaky croissant layers
White Sugar ¼ cup Adds sweetness
Butter 3 tbsp melted Adds richness and flavour
Cream Cheese 100 g Creates a smooth creamy filling
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp Adds extra flavour
Egg 1 Creates a golden pastry finish
Lemon Juice 1 tbsp Adds a fresh, tangy flavour
Sweet raspberries, creamy filling, and golden buttery pastry come together to create a delicious, fruity, and flaky raspberry croissant! 🍓🥐✨

🍓 How to Make Raspberry Croissants 🍓

Step 1: Prepare the Raspberry Filling

Place the raspberries, sugar, and lemon juice into a small saucepan. Cook gently until the raspberries soften and the mixture becomes slightly thick. Allow it to cool.

Step 2: Prepare the Pastry

Place the puff pastry onto a lightly floured surface. Cut the pastry into long triangles, ready to be shaped into croissants.

Step 3: Add the Filling

Spread a small amount of cream cheese near the wide end of each pastry triangle. Add a spoonful of the cooled raspberry filling.

Step 4: Roll the Croissants

Starting from the wide end, carefully roll each pastry triangle towards the pointed end. Curve the ends slightly to create a classic croissant shape.

Step 5: Bake

Brush the tops with beaten egg and bake at 200°C for approximately 15–20 minutes, or until the croissants are puffed and golden brown. Allow them to cool slightly before serving.

✨ Enjoy a flaky, buttery raspberry croissant filled with sweet raspberries and creamy goodness! 🍓🥐💗✨
