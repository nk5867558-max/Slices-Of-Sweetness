🍑 Peach Pie 🍑

Warm, buttery, fruity, and filled with sweet juicy peaches!

🍑 ⭐ 🍑 ✨ 🍑 ⭐ 🥧 🧡 🍑 ✨ 🧡 🥧

A warm, buttery peach pie filled with sweet, juicy peaches and a hint of cinnamon. The golden, flaky crust surrounds a soft and fruity peach filling, creating a cosy homemade dessert that is perfect for sharing with family and friends. Every slice is sweet, comforting, and full of delicious peach flavour.

🍑 Key Ingredients 🍑

Ingredients Measurements Use
Fresh Peaches 6 cups sliced Main flavour and filling
All-Purpose Flour 2½ cups Creates the pie crust
Granulated Sugar ¾ cup Adds sweetness
Brown Sugar ¼ cup Adds a rich caramel flavour
Butter ½ cup Makes the crust rich and flaky
Cinnamon 1 tsp Adds warm spice flavour
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp Enhances the peach flavour
Cornstarch 3 tbsp Thickens the peach filling
Egg 1 Helps create a golden crust
Sweet juicy peaches, warm cinnamon, and a golden buttery crust come together to make this cosy peach pie absolutely delicious! 🍑🥧

🍑 How to Make Peach Pie 🍑

Step 1: Prepare the Peaches

Wash, peel, and slice the peaches. Place them in a large bowl and add the sugar, brown sugar, cinnamon, vanilla extract, and cornstarch. Mix gently until the peaches are evenly coated.

Step 2: Prepare the Pie Crust

Combine the flour and butter in a bowl. Mix until the mixture becomes crumbly. Gradually add cold water and mix until a dough forms. Be careful not to overmix the dough.

Step 3: Assemble the Pie

Roll out half of the dough and place it into a pie dish. Pour the peach filling into the crust and spread it evenly. Roll out the remaining dough and place it over the peaches. Seal the edges and make a few small cuts in the top crust to allow steam to escape.

Step 4: Add the Egg Wash

Beat the egg and lightly brush it over the top of the pie crust. This helps the crust become golden brown and gives it a beautiful baked finish.

Step 5: Bake

Bake the peach pie at 190°C (375°F) for about 45–55 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the peach filling is bubbling.

Step 6: Cool and Serve

Allow the pie to cool for at least 20–30 minutes before slicing. This gives the peach filling time to thicken. Serve warm on its own or with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

✨ Enjoy a warm slice of buttery, flaky peach pie filled with sweet juicy peaches! 🍑🥧✨
