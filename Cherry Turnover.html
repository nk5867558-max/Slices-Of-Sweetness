🍒🥐 Cherry Turnover 🥐🍒

Flaky, golden pastry filled with sweet and juicy cherries! ❤️

🍒 ⭐ 🍒 ✨ 🍒 ❤️ 🥐 🌸 🍒 ✨ ❤️ 🥐

A delicious cherry turnover made with flaky, golden pastry and a sweet filling of juicy cherries. The pastry is crisp and buttery on the outside while the warm cherry filling is soft, fruity, and bursting with flavour. Every bite is sweet, buttery, and perfect for breakfast or an afternoon treat! 🍒🥐✨

🍒 Key Ingredients 🍒

Ingredients Measurements Use
Fresh Cherries 2 cups Main flavour and filling
Puff Pastry 1 sheet Creates the flaky pastry
White Sugar ½ cup Adds sweetness
Cornstarch 2 tbsp Thickens the cherry filling
Butter 2 tbsp melted Adds richness and flavour
Lemon Juice 1 tbsp Adds a fresh, tangy flavour
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp Adds extra flavour
Egg 1 Creates a golden pastry finish
Sweet cherries, buttery pastry, and a golden flaky crust come together to create a delicious, fruity, and comforting cherry turnover! 🍒🥐✨

🍒 How to Make Cherry Turnovers 🍒

Step 1: Prepare the Cherry Filling

Remove the pits from the cherries and place them in a saucepan. Add the sugar, cornstarch, and lemon juice. Cook over medium heat until the cherries soften and the mixture thickens.

Step 2: Prepare the Pastry

Place the puff pastry onto a lightly floured surface. Cut the pastry into equal-sized squares for the turnovers.

Step 3: Add the Cherry Filling

Place a spoonful of the cooled cherry filling in the centre of each pastry square. Be careful not to overfill the pastry.

Step 4: Fold the Turnovers

Fold each pastry square diagonally to form a triangle. Press the edges together with a fork to seal the filling inside. Brush the tops with beaten egg.

Step 5: Bake

Bake at 200°C for approximately 15–20 minutes, or until the pastry is puffed and golden brown. Allow the turnovers to cool slightly before serving.

✨ Enjoy a warm, flaky cherry turnover filled with sweet, juicy cherries and buttery golden pastry! 🍒🥐❤️✨
