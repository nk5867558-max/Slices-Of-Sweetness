🍌 Banana Bread 🍌

Soft, moist, and packed with sweet banana flavour!

🍌 ⭐ 🍌 ✨ 🍌 ⭐ 🥐 🤎 🍌 ✨ 🤎 🍞

Freshly baked banana bread with a soft, moist texture and rich banana flavour. Made with ripe bananas and simple ingredients, this cosy café-style treat is sweet, fluffy, and perfect for breakfast, dessert, or an afternoon snack. Every slice is warm, comforting, and packed with homemade goodness.

🍌 Key Ingredients 🍌

Ingredients Measurements Use
Ripe Bananas 3 medium Main flavour and moisture
All-Purpose Flour 1½ cups Creates structure
Baking Soda 1 tsp Helps the bread rise
Brown Sugar ¾ cup Adds sweetness and flavour
Egg 1 Binds ingredients together
Butter ½ cup melted Adds richness and moisture
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp Extra flavour
Cinnamon (Optional) ½ tsp Warm spice flavour
Sweet bananas, warm cinnamon, and buttery goodness make this banana bread soft, fluffy, and absolutely delicious!

🍌 How to Make Banana Bread 🍌

Step 1: Prepare the Bananas

Mash the ripe bananas in a large bowl until smooth. The riper the bananas are, the sweeter and more flavourful your banana bread will be.

Step 2: Mix the Wet Ingredients

Add the melted butter, brown sugar, egg, and vanilla extract. Mix until everything is smooth and well combined.

Step 3: Add the Dry Ingredients

Add the flour, baking soda, and cinnamon. Stir gently until no dry flour remains. Avoid overmixing the batter.

Step 4: Bake

Pour the batter into a greased loaf pan. Bake at 175°C (350°F) for 50–60 minutes or until a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean.

Step 5: Cool and Serve

Allow the banana bread to cool for 10–15 minutes before slicing. Enjoy warm on its own or with a little butter.

✨ Enjoy a warm slice of soft, moist banana bread packed with sweet banana flavour and homemade comfort! 🍌🍞✨
