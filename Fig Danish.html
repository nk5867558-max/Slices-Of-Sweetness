🍇🥐 Fig Danish 🥐🍇

Flaky, buttery, and filled with sweet, juicy figs! 💜

🍇 ⭐ 🍇 ✨ 🍇 💜 🥐 🌸 🍇 ✨ 💜 🥐

A delicious Fig Danish made with flaky, buttery pastry, creamy filling, and sweet, juicy figs. The golden pastry is crisp on the outside and soft inside, while the figs add a naturally sweet and fruity flavour. Every bite is buttery, creamy, fruity, and perfect for breakfast or an afternoon treat! 🍇🥐💜

🍇 Key Ingredients 🍇

Ingredients Measurements Use
Fresh Figs 4–5 Main fruity flavour and topping
Puff Pastry 1 sheet Creates the flaky Danish pastry
Cream Cheese 150 g Creates the creamy filling
White Sugar ¼ cup Adds sweetness
Egg 1 Helps bind the filling
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp Adds extra flavour
Honey 2 tbsp Adds sweetness to the figs
Lemon Juice 1 tbsp Adds a fresh, zesty flavour
Flaky pastry, creamy filling, and sweet juicy figs come together to create a delicious, fruity, and buttery Danish! 🍇🥐✨

🍇 How to Make Fig Danish 🍇

Step 1: Prepare the Filling

Beat the cream cheese, sugar, vanilla extract, and lemon juice together until smooth and creamy.

Step 2: Prepare the Pastry

Place the puff pastry onto a lined baking tray. Cut the pastry into squares or rectangles and gently fold the edges to create a border.

Step 3: Add the Cream Filling

Spoon a small amount of the cream cheese mixture into the centre of each pastry piece. Spread it gently without covering the pastry border.

Step 4: Add the Figs

Slice the fresh figs into thin pieces and arrange them over the cream cheese filling. Drizzle a little honey over the figs.

Step 5: Bake

Bake at 200°C for approximately 15–20 minutes, or until the pastry is puffed and golden brown. Allow the Danish to cool slightly before serving.

✨ Enjoy a flaky, buttery Fig Danish filled with creamy cheese and sweet, juicy figs! 🍇🥐💜✨
