🍎🥧 Apple Strudel 🥧🍎

A warm, flaky pastry filled with sweet apples and cinnamon!

🍎 ⭐ 🍎 ✨ 🍎 🤎 🥧 🍂 🍎 ✨ 🤎 🥧

A delicious apple strudel made with flaky, golden pastry and a warm filling of sweet, juicy apples. Filled with cinnamon, sugar, and a hint of buttery goodness, this comforting dessert is crisp on the outside and soft and fruity on the inside. Every bite is warm, sweet, and perfect for an afternoon treat! 🍎🥧✨

🍎 Key Ingredients 🍎

Ingredients Measurements Use
Apples 4 medium Main flavour and filling
Puff Pastry 1 sheet Creates the flaky pastry
White Sugar ½ cup Adds sweetness
Brown Sugar ¼ cup Adds rich caramel flavour
Butter 3 tbsp melted Adds richness and flavour
Cinnamon 1 tsp Adds warm spice flavour
Breadcrumbs ½ cup Helps absorb extra apple juices
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp Adds extra flavour
Sweet apples, warm cinnamon, and golden flaky pastry come together to create a delicious, comforting, and homemade apple strudel! 🍎🥧✨

🍎 How to Make Apple Strudel 🍎

Step 1: Prepare the Apples

Peel and thinly slice the apples. Place them in a large bowl and mix with the white sugar, brown sugar, cinnamon, and vanilla extract.

Step 2: Prepare the Pastry

Place the puff pastry onto a lined baking tray. Gently roll it out so it is large enough to hold the apple filling.

Step 3: Add the Filling

Sprinkle the breadcrumbs over the centre of the pastry. Place the apple mixture on top and spread it evenly, leaving space around the edges.

Step 4: Roll the Strudel

Fold the pastry over the apple filling and carefully roll it into a log shape. Seal the edges and brush the top with melted butter.

Step 5: Bake

Bake at 190°C for approximately 35–40 minutes, or until the pastry is golden brown and crisp. Allow the strudel to cool slightly before serving.

✨ Enjoy a warm slice of flaky apple strudel filled with sweet apples, buttery pastry, and delicious cinnamon flavour! 🍎🥧🤎✨
