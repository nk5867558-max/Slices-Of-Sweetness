🫐 Blueberry Cheesecake 🫐

Creamy, rich, and bursting with sweet blueberry flavour! 💙

🫐 ⭐ 🫐 ✨ 🫐 💙 🧁 💜 🫐 ✨ 💙

A creamy and delicious blueberry cheesecake with a smooth, rich filling and a buttery biscuit base. Topped with juicy blueberries and sweet blueberry sauce, this dreamy dessert is the perfect combination of creamy, fruity, and sweet. Every slice is soft, refreshing, and packed with blueberry goodness! 🫐💙

🫐 Key Ingredients 🫐

Ingredients Measurements Use
Blueberries 2 cups Main fruity flavour
Cream Cheese 500 g Creates the creamy filling
Digestive Biscuits 250 g Creates the cheesecake base
Butter 100 g melted Binds the biscuit base
White Sugar ¾ cup Adds sweetness
Eggs 2 Helps set the cheesecake
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp Adds extra flavour
Lemon Juice 2 tbsp Adds a fresh, slightly tangy flavour
Creamy cheesecake, juicy blueberries, and a buttery biscuit base come together to create a sweet, fruity, and delicious dessert! 🫐🍰✨

🫐 How to Make Blueberry Cheesecake 🫐

Step 1: Prepare the Biscuit Base

Crush the digestive biscuits into fine crumbs. Mix the crumbs with melted butter until everything is well combined. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom of a lined cake tin.

Step 2: Make the Cheesecake Filling

Beat the cream cheese and sugar together until smooth and creamy. Add the eggs, vanilla extract, and lemon juice. Mix until everything is fully combined and smooth.

Step 3: Add the Blueberries

Gently fold some of the blueberries into the cheesecake mixture. Save some blueberries for decorating the top of the cheesecake.

Step 4: Bake

Pour the cheesecake mixture over the biscuit base. Bake at 160°C for approximately 50–60 minutes, or until the centre is almost set.

Step 5: Cool and Decorate

Allow the cheesecake to cool completely before placing it in the fridge. Chill for at least 4 hours, or overnight. Top with fresh blueberries and blueberry sauce before serving.

✨ Enjoy a creamy slice of blueberry cheesecake with a buttery base and delicious fresh blueberry flavour! 🫐🍰💙✨
